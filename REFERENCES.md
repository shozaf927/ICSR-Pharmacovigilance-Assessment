# References and Further Reading

Always verify the current official version, local adoption and effective date before operational use.

## ICH and regulatory guidance
1. **ICH E2A — Clinical Safety Data Management: Definitions and Standards for Expedited Reporting.** FDA/ICH, 1995.  
   https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/e2a-clinical-safety-data-management-definitions-and-standards-expedited-reporting

2. **ICH E2D(R1) — Post-Approval Safety Data: Definitions and Standards for Management and Reporting of Individual Case Safety Reports.** Final Step 4, adopted 15 September 2025.  
   https://database.ich.org/sites/default/files/ICH_E2D%28R1%29_Step4_FinalGuideline_2025_0819.pdf  
   EMA guideline page: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/scientific-guidelines/ich-e2d-post-approval-safety-data-management

3. **ICH E2B(R3) — ICSR Specification and Related Files.**  
   https://admin.ich.org/node/348

4. **FDA — E2B(R3) Electronic Transmission of Individual Case Safety Reports Implementation Guide.**  
   https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/e2br3-electronic-transmission-individual-case-safety-reports-implementation-guide-data

5. **CDSCO — Guidance Document: Periodic Safety Update Reports / Pharmacovigilance.**  
   https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/export/sites/CDSCO_WEB/Pdf-documents/PSUR_PV_AEFI/Guidance_Documentpsur2.pdf  
   The guidance cited in this project specifies MAH timelines of 15 calendar days for serious and 90 calendar days for non-serious adverse events to CDSCO and NCC-PvPI. Confirm current applicability and channels.

6. **Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI), Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission — Toolkit.**  
   https://ipc.gov.in/PvPI/toolkit.html

## Operational notes
- MedDRA and WHODrug are controlled/licensed terminologies. Never invent numeric codes.
- This project does not reproduce the full E2B(R3) data dictionary, CIOMS-I form or proprietary dictionary content.
- Regional law, product-specific RSI, company SOPs and validated safety systems take precedence over this educational summary.
