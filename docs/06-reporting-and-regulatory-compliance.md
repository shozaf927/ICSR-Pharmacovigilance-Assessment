# Reporting and Regulatory Compliance

## Identify the pathway first
Before calculating a deadline determine whether the case is post-marketing or clinical-trial, the product/region, validity, seriousness, suspected relationship, expectedness, applicable RSI, Day 0 and current local requirements. Do not apply one jurisdiction’s timeline globally.

## India: MAH post-marketing guidance
The CDSCO guidance cited in this project specifies that MAHs report:
- Serious adverse events within **15 calendar days** of receipt from any source to CDSCO and NCC-PvPI.
- Non-serious adverse events within **90 calendar days** of receipt from any source to CDSCO and NCC-PvPI.
It also discusses lack of efficacy, medication error and other report types. Confirm current operative guidance, product applicability, submission channels and SOPs before a real submission. This is an India-specific MAH pathway, not a universal global rule.

## ICH E2D(R1)
ICH E2D(R1), adopted at ICH Step 4 in September 2025, updates post-approval safety data definitions and standards, including newer information sources. The guideline describes expedited ICSR reporting according to local/regional criteria, generally as soon as possible and no later than 15 calendar days after Day 0; non-expedited timelines may vary. Regional law and implementation control.

## Clinical-trial SUSAR
A SUSAR must be:
1. Suspected (reasonable possibility of causal relationship).
2. Unexpected against applicable RSI.
3. Serious.

ICH E2A clinical-trial expedited reporting framework generally specifies:
- Fatal/life-threatening unexpected suspected adverse reactions: as soon as possible, no later than 7 calendar days, with follow-up information within the additional period required by the applicable framework.
- Other serious, unexpected suspected adverse reactions: no later than 15 calendar days.

Exact recipients, clock triggers and follow-up requirements depend on current jurisdictional law. EU CTR/CTIS and other national frameworks must be checked separately. Serious but expected reactions are not SUSARs; serious events without suspected relationship are not SUSARs, although SAE collection/reporting may still apply.

## Periodic reports and formats
PBRER/PSUR is an aggregate periodic benefit-risk evaluation, not a substitute for individual expedited ICSR reporting. CIOMS-I is a widely used structured form; E2B(R3) is the electronic ICSR message standard. Use regional implementation guides and retain submission acknowledgements/reconciliation.
