## 10. Draft narrative
A 34-year-old female patient (A.B.) received one oral dose of amoxicillin/clavulanate 625 mg, reportedly for acute bacterial sinusitis. Approximately 15 minutes after administration, she developed widespread pruritic wheals, lip/tongue swelling, wheezing and dyspnoea. At emergency assessment, blood pressure was 78/46 mmHg and oxygen saturation was 89% on room air. The emergency physician diagnosed anaphylaxis. The patient was treated with intramuscular epinephrine, oxygen, intravenous fluids, antihistamine and corticosteroid therapy. The suspect product was discontinued. She was admitted for inpatient observation for approximately 24 hours and was reported to have recovered with no sequelae at initial report. No rechallenge was performed. No known drug allergy was recorded in the available chart; detailed prior antibiotic exposure and concomitant medication history were unavailable. Product brand, manufacturer, batch/lot, exact dose timing, full hospital records and laboratory information were not reported and are requested for follow-up.
