# Educational and Data Protection Disclaimer

This repository is an educational simulation. All case facts are fictional. Nothing here is a real patient safety report, validated regulatory submission, medical advice or legal advice.

Do not upload identifiable patient information, medical records, reporter contact details or confidential company safety data to a public repository. Use synthetic or properly de-identified information only.

Before workplace use:
- Verify current ICH and local regulatory requirements.
- Verify product-specific RSI and local label version.
- Use current licensed MedDRA and WHODrug releases.
- Follow approved company SOPs, safety database workflows and medical review.
- Confirm reporting recipients, electronic submission channels and due dates.
